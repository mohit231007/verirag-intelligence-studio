Retail Promotion Policy - Demonstration Document
Effective date: 1 September 2026

Frozen foods promotional window

The standard promotional window for frozen-food products begins on 15 October 2026 and ends on 28 November 2026. Participating suppliers must register eligible products before the campaign begins.

Early booking incentive

Suppliers that confirm inventory and promotional funding at least 30 calendar days before the campaign start receive an additional five percent co-marketing credit. The credit applies to approved campaign media only and is not a reduction in the wholesale unit price.

Approval controls

Every campaign requires written approval from the category manager and finance business partner. A supplier email alone is not approval. Changes after approval must be recorded in the campaign register before publication.
